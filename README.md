# Numerical-Investigation-of-Passive-Split-and-Recombine-SAR-Micromixers-
– Conducted a comparative numerical study of three passive micromixer geometries
    (Straight, Circular, and Rhombus) at low Reynolds numbers.                                                                                                    – Solved incompressible Navier–Stokes and Convection–Diffusion equations using
    ANSYS FLUENT to analyze fluid flow and species transport.                                                                                                          – Quantified mixing performance using the Mixing Index (M.I.) derived from mass-fraction
    variance across 400 sample points at the outlet.                                                                                                                            – Compared mixing efficiencies and concentration distributions to identify the most
    effective micromixer geometry.
